# Migration boundary from YouTube_pepline

The old FactForge system is used only as a source of lessons and approved raw inputs. The following are not migrated:

- multi-stage manifest state machine
- Claude skill/orchestrator graph
- duplicated producer/QA gates
- render-only derived scene plans that can drift
- temporary probe or diagnostic workflows
- scripts tied to one video or VPS incident
- generated render projects committed per film

Eligible inputs may be copied only after verification:

- original narration recordings
- licensed footage and music with provenance
- official source captures and research catalogs
- approved visual identity decisions
- factual claims and citations

Every migrated asset must be referenced by the canonical plan and pass integrity/provenance checks.
